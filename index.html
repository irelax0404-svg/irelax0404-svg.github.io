Final Whistle Uploader

Private publishing tool for our own YouTube channels.

Final Whistle Uploader is a small, private tool used by the owner of the football channels Final Whistle Edits and Bola na Rede to upload short football ranking videos (Shorts) to their own YouTube channels on a schedule.

What it does

  • Uploads videos that we produce to our own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads basic statistics (view counts) of our own uploaded videos to decide which topics perform best.

The tool is not offered to the public and is only used with channels we own.
