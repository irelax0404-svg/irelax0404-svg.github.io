Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

This policy describes how Final Whistle Uploader ("the app") handles data.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It does not have public users.

Data we access

  • YouTube upload permission (youtube.upload): used only to upload videos to the owner's channels.
  • YouTube read-only permission (youtube.readonly): used only to read view counts of the owner's own uploaded videos.

How data is used and stored

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold or transferred to third parties. Video statistics are stored locally only to choose future video topics. No personal data of other people is collected.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
